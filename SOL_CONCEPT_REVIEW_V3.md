# Sol independent concept review V3

Reviewed all **115** queued bilingual trainer-name proposals independently from the two pinned local TCGdex TypeScript card files for each row. The repository HEAD is `a211f11f3ba57d56c65c5ac33be35b77d3280757`. All **230** referenced file reads matched their provenance SHA-256, and all **115** canonical source-snapshot hashes matched. For every row, both source names, Trainer category, and subtype were checked. Effect text was read where available.

## Verdicts

- Approve: **34**
- Reject: **81**
- Uncertain: **0**

These verdicts concern **bilingual name concepts only**. They do not verify that two individual cards are the same printing or suitable price comparables. The row-specific rationale and digest are in `catalog/concept-review-v3-sol.csv`.

## Evidence exceptions

- The Japanese legacy `ポケモンセンター` record omits effect text and subtype. Its English `Pokémon Center` counterpart has a healing effect. This is an approval of the name concept, with the text limitation recorded in the CSV.
- The two rows involving the Japanese legacy `ポケモンセンター` record are the only rows with an omitted effect. The `Challenge!` English card has no explicit subtype, while its Japanese candidates are Stadium cards; both are rejected on name and effect.
- `プレイヤーズセレモニー` and `おいわいファンファーレ` are distinct Japanese Stadium concepts despite sharing an illustrator and partial draw/turn-ending mechanics with unrelated `Challenge!`.
- `アクア団の幹部`, `マグマ団の幹部`, and `アクア団のしたっぱ` do not denote `Phoebe`; all three are rejected.
- No official web source was needed: the pinned card-specific records resolved the name-concept decisions, with omissions explicitly noted.
