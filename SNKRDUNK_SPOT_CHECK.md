The SNKRDUNK product page is a **card family**, while its price tiers split by raw condition A–D and by grader/grade (for example PSA 10, PSA 9, BGS and ARS). The headline “¥…~” is a minimum starting ask across available tiers. It must not be compared with a PSA 10 ask or treated as a sale price. A listing page is narrower and can carry quantity, condition and its own ask. The canonical card match therefore uses set code and collector number; the priceable unit also needs condition or grader/grade, quantity and listing ID.

The three page matches are a small manual check, not a measured match rate. This pilot has no SNKRDUNK listing observations, no current ask series and no verified paid prices. The fields and use rights needed to build those series are specified in [SNKRDUNK_ACCESS_REQUEST.md](SNKRDUNK_ACCESS_REQUEST.md).
